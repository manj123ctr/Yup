      How to stewardRead me
      Drag with the Fence tool to build a barrier. It snaps to walls and other barriers. Barriers cost budget, unless Admin mode is on. Tap any gate to open or close it, even mid-event. Green lamps mean open, red means closed. The Gate tool builds new ones. Red rings mean someone is being crushed. Pressure travels through bodies: the people at the front carry the push of everyone behind them. Tap Crush to see where it builds. People who fall get trampled unless the crowd around them eases off. Overloaded barriers snap. Deaths never stop the event, it runs to the end, but every death costs you stars. Rival fans fight when they touch. Keep them apart with barriers, gates or timing. Ambulances and police come for the injured and the dead. Their crews carry people out on stretchers, but they can be crushed and trampled like anyone else. The Hollow Hand calls before every event and offers money. Take it and their bomber walks into your crowd. The money is yours, but the police investigate. Zoom with the mouse wheel or two fingers. Pan with a right-drag or two fingers.
